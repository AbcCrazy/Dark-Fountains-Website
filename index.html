What are Dark Fountains?

Dark Fountains are a gateway to another dimension that any being can create. They are pillars of darkness that form beings out of ordinary objects. Dark Fountains create beings known as 'darkners'. These beings are formed when it becomes darker than dark. Each darkner has its own personality and ability, depending on the object it originated from.


How Dark Fountains are good for the enviroment

Despite the controversy, Dark Fountains are required in order to sustain a balance between light and dark. The world cannot exist with too much of both, and if the balance were to be disturbed, the Roaring will begin, and the earth will draw her final breath.


Why we want to bring the Roaring

Despite all this bad stuff that could happen, we want you to help us bring the Roaring. At Roaring Industries, we believe that your safety comes last, so we've spent years of hard work and dedication trying to come up with a plan on how we can begin the Roaring, and end all life on earth.
