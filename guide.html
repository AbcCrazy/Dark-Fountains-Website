Guide to Creating Dark Fountains

This is a simple step-by-step guide on how you can create your own Dark Fountains!


1) Obtain a sharp object, preferably a knife.

2) Concentrate your will into the blade, or pointy end of said object.

3) Stab the earth with your desired object so that the pointy end collides with the ground.

4) Enjoy your newly created Dark Fountain
